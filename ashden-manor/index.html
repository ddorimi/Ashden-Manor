THE MANOR

Ashden, in good light

A Georgian house in the English countryside, with the whole place set aside for you and your guests. No one else checking in, and no one else's wedding next door.

THE CEREMONY ROOM

Say it in a room made for it

Our indoor ceremony room is bright, calm and close, so everyone you invited can see and hear every word. Keep it simple or fill it with flowers, it's yours to set up.

THE GARDENS

Drinks on the lawn

Manicured gardens for drinks, photographs and a slow wander between courses. With nobody else on site, your photos stay yours.

THE FOOD

Shared tables, passed around

Big plates, warm bread and plenty to go round. Menus are built around what you and your guests love to eat, from £95 per head.

GETTING READY

No rush, no crowd

A private dressing room where you can get ready in your own time, with just the people you want beside you.

THE EVENING

The party after dark

When the lights come on, the party carries on in a house that's still all yours. Dance, talk and stay up late with nobody else to think about.

THE DINING ROOM

Long dinners, long tables

Our Georgian dining room is made for sitting down together. Good plates, a long table and a whole evening of food cooked in our own kitchen.
